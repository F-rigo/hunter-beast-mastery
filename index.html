Beast Mastery Hunter Guide

Beast Mastery Hunter for World of Warcraft: Forever, from level 1 to 60, with the DPS rotation and pet notes. Sources read on November 17, 2026, almost all from Wowhead Forever.

Wowhead Forever is the main source for this guide. Its talent pages show builds as calculator images, so the talent point order below comes from the earlier Hunter research (Mobalytics and Leprestore) and is marked by source. Items marked Sources differ list each source's version. Items marked Not confirmed were not found in a source I could read.

1. Overview and changes in Forever

  • Beast Mastery Hunters get Summon Hawk, a talent that works like a second, temporary pet. Only 2 hawks can be active at once, and it shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot. Confirmed by Wowhead (overview, November 14, 2026).
  • Aimed Shot is now baseline (not a talent). It shares a cooldown with Multi-Shot and deals an extra 350 ranged damage. Confirmed by Wowhead.
  • Focused Fire (talent): +2% damage for you and your pet while the pet is active. Endurance Training: +15% pet health and armor. Intimidation: pet stun for 3 seconds on its next hit, with 100% extra crit on that hit. Confirmed by Wowhead.
  • Auto Shot has a 0.5 second cast time again, and it can continue during other casts. Confirmed by Wowhead.
  • Wowhead rates Beast Mastery as having high burst and strong kiting, but no group utility. Its viability for raids is questionable, and the sources say it relies on high base damage.

2. Pets: how they scale

Pets now take part of your stats. Wowhead lists these rules (Confirmed):

  • Stamina: each point of your Stamina gives the pet 2 health.
  • Armor: the pet gains 30% of your armor.
  • Attack Power: the pet gains 10% of your highest Attack Power source.
  • Critical strike: the pet gains 100% of your crit chance.
  • Pets can no longer be buffed by other classes, and they have avoidance now.

This is why Lethal Attacks, Careful Aim and Trueshot Aura also raise pet damage. Wowhead says Improved Tracking is slightly stronger than Mortal Shots because it is a blanket damage increase.

Leveling pets (Wowhead)

  • Cat and Raptor: highest damage, with a 1.1x damage modifier. Both have Claw, Bite and Dash. Cat has high attack speed variants; Raptor does not.
  • Carrion Bird: 1.0x damage modifier, with Claw, Bite and Dive. Its Demoralizing Screech greatly weakens the enemy it fights.
  • About every 10 levels, catch a pet with higher ranks of Claw and Bite at a Stable Master, then teach the new ranks with Beast Training.

Sources differ on pet rankings. The earlier pet research (Axeetech, Exitlag) put Carrion Bird and Wolf as S tier and Cat as S tier, and Bear as the best for Beast Mastery leveling in Exitlag. Wowhead does not publish a tier list.

4. Talent builds for endgame (Wowhead)

PvE dungeons and raids: Lethal Attacks, Careful Aim and Trueshot Aura, which all raise pet damage through stat transfer. Improved Tracking over Mortal Shots, since it is a blanket damage increase. Untaken Beast Mastery talents do not add damage.

PvP: Improved Concussive Shot (for a lucky stun), Improved Aspect of the Monkey against melee, and Summon Hawk, which has no minimum range, so you can cast it while fighting melee.

Wowhead's talent builds page was last updated November 16, 2026. Exact points per talent are in its calculator, which I could not read.

7. Rotation and cooldowns (Wowhead)

Single target (priority order)

  1. Keep Hunter's Mark on the target.
  2. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  3. Use Summon Hawk (keep 2 hawks rolling).
  4. Cast Arcane Shot every third rotation, in place of Summon Hawk.
  5. Use Aimed Shot.

AoE

Wowhead says the AoE rotation is the same priority list with Multi-Shot added. Until level 40, use a more basic AoE rotation. Not confirmed the exact AoE order, because the page's AoE tab was not readable.

Cooldowns

  • DPS cooldowns: Rapid Fire and Bestial Wrath. Use them whenever they are up, ideally with an on-use trinket or racial.
  • Utility: Tranquilizing Shot for enrage, Freezing Trap and Frost Trap for adds, Viper Sting for mana drain, and Intimidation whenever there is a target to stun.

Confirmed by Wowhead (October 30, 2026).

8. Professions for leveling and endgame (Wowhead)

  • Leveling: Skinning and Leatherworking. Skin every beast you kill, then use the leather to craft Leatherworking gear. New Bind-on-Pickup Leatherworking pieces are strong for their levels. Confirmed
  • Endgame: Engineering gives strong guns, headwear, grenades and mines. Wowhead says it basically needs Mining. Replace Skinning with Engineering once you can afford the materials. Confirmed
  • Alchemy and Enchanting: Not confirmed how strong they are.

9. Camping and consumables for leveling (Wowhead)

  • Camp buffs that always help a BM Hunter: Fish Bowl, Enchanted Lute, Camp Chair, Mana Well. Camp Tent is a nice addition and speeds leveling slightly.
  • Food: any food that adds experience from kills (5%) is worth it. Choose food that adds Attack Power or Agility, and food your pet can eat.
  • Bandages: learn First Aid with the cloth you find. Bandage tiers run from Linen to Runecloth.

10. Legacy Points (Wowhead)

  • Bountiful Harvest: more rare materials from Skinning, for extra gold.
  • Thrill of Adventure and Talented: more longevity and strength in the field, which makes leveling faster.
  • Gourmand: longer food buff, which keeps the 5% experience boost up longer.

What I could not confirm

  • Talent point ranks for levels 20 to 24 and 40 to 60: Wowhead's builds are calculator images, and the earlier sources disagree.
  • The AoE rotation order: the AoE tab was not readable in the browser.
  • Pet tier rankings: Wowhead does not publish one, and the other sites disagree.
  • Ruthless crit: Wowhead's overview says 1%, a reader comment says 2%.
  • Plainsrunning maximum: Wowhead's Hunter page gives no number; the Warrior page says 60%.
